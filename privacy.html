Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy describes how DramaCuts Uploader ("the application") handles information when it connects to Google APIs. The application is a personal, single-user command-line tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel it uploads to.

What the application does

The application runs locally on the operator's computer. Its only purpose is to upload video files chosen by the operator to that operator's own YouTube channel, and to read back the resulting video status (video ID, privacy status, upload status and processing status).

Google user data accessed

With the operator's explicit OAuth consent, the application requests these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload - to upload videos to the authorized channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly - to read the channel identity and the status of videos that were uploaded.

It reads only the authorized channel's basic identity (channel ID, title, handle) and the metadata of videos uploaded by the application itself. It does not read messages, comments, playlists, analytics, contacts, or any other Google user data, and it does not access data belonging to any other user.

How data is stored and used

  • Video files remain on the operator's local computer and are uploaded only to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the local computer, in a file readable solely by the operator's OS account. They are never transmitted to any third party or server operated by the developer.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising, profiling, machine learning, or any purpose other than performing the upload requested by the operator.
  • The application has no server-side component, no database, no telemetry, and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

Retention and deletion

The operator controls all data. Deleting the local token file immediately revokes the application's ability to call Google APIs, and access can also be revoked at any time from the operator's Google Account permissions page. Uploaded videos can be deleted from YouTube Studio. No copy of user data is retained by the developer elsewhere.

Limited Use disclosure

DramaCuts Uploader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
